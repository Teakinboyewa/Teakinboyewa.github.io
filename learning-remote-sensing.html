17-week course

Remote Sensing

From the basics of satellite imagery to forest mapping, LiDAR, and machine learning, using QGIS, Google Earth Engine (GEE), and Python.

About this course

This course teaches you to turn satellite and LiDAR data into maps and measurements of forests. It starts with the physics of remote sensing and the basics of GIS, then works through Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery, vegetation indices, classification, and change detection.

In the second half, you move to cloud computing with Google Earth Engine and to machine learning in Python. You then apply these skills to map forest waste and model biomass, and finish with a capstone research project.

How it is organized

  • 1–8
    Foundations and optical imageryQGIS
  • 9–11
    Cloud computing, LiDAR, and biomassGoogle Earth Engine
  • 12–14
    Machine learningPython
  • 15–17
    Applications and capstoneGEE + Python
