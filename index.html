About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Geography at The Pennsylvania State University, where I work as a Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant. I expect to graduate in Spring 2027.

My work sits where GeoAI meets autonomous GIS. I lead development of GIS Co-Scientist, a human-AI framework that forms hypotheses and runs spatial analyses, and of SpatialAnalysisAgent, an AI agent plugin for QGIS. I also use big data on how people visit places to study neighborhood health.

Before Penn State, I earned an M.S. in Remote Sensing and GIS and a B.S. in Surveying and Geoinformatics from the Federal University of Technology, Akure, Nigeria. I also worked as a field surveyor and GIS analyst. In 2026 I was an AI Research Intern at bAI Labs.
