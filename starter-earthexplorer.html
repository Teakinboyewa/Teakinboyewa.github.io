Download Landsat from EarthExplorer

Create a free USGS account and download a Landsat scene.

Time: about 30 minutesLevel: complete beginner

Watch

“How to download Landsat 9 images from EarthExplorer USGS” by GIS Educator. Watch on YouTube.

1. Create a free account

  1. Go to ers.cr.usgs.gov/register and fill in the form. It asks a few questions about you and how you will use the data. Students can choose education.
  2. Confirm your account from the email USGS sends you.

2. Search for a scene

  1. Go to earthexplorer.usgs.gov and click Login.
  2. In the Search Criteria tab, type a place name in the search box, or click on the map to drop points around your area.
  3. Set a date range. Then set cloud cover to 0–10%, so you only see clear images.
  4. Click Data Sets. Open Landsat › Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 and tick Landsat 8-9 OLI/TIRS C2 L2.
  5. Click Results.

3. Choose and download

  1. Each result has small icons. The footprint icon shows the scene's outline on the map, and the browse icon shows a preview picture. Use them to pick a clear scene that covers your area.
  2. Click the download icon (an arrow pointing down).
  3. Choose the full product bundle, or pick only the files you need. Week 3 lists which files to download.
  4. Unzip the download into your week's data folder. Landsat files come as .tar; see Organize your files and folders for how to unzip them.

Common problems

  • No results. Widen the date range, raise the cloud cover limit, or check that you ticked the data set.
  • The download button does nothing. Make sure you are logged in, and allow pop-ups for the site.
  • The file is huge. A full Landsat scene is about 1 GB. Download only the bands you need.
