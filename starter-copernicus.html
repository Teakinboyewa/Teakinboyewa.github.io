Explore Sentinel-2 in Copernicus Browser

View and download free Sentinel-2 images in your web browser.

Time: about 25 minutesLevel: complete beginner

Watch

“How to Download Sentinel-2 Satellite Imagery from Copernicus Browser: Step-by-Step Tutorial (2026)” by Arnav Saxena. Watch on YouTube.

The Copernicus Browser lets you view Sentinel-2 images of anywhere on Earth right in your web browser. You do not need to install anything.

1. Create a free account

  1. Go to browser.dataspace.copernicus.eu.
  2. Click Login, then Register, and fill in the form. Confirm your email.

2. Find an image

  1. Type a place name in the search box at the top, or zoom in on the map.
  2. In the search panel on the left, choose Sentinel-2, level L2A (corrected for the atmosphere).
  3. Set the maximum cloud cover (for example 10%) and a date range, then search.
  4. Click a result to show it on the map.

3. Try different views

In the Visualize panel, you can switch between ready-made views. Start with True color (what your eyes would see) and False color (healthy plants appear bright red). Week 1 explains why.

4. Download

Use the download button on the right-hand toolbar to save what you see. You can save a simple picture, or an analytical image with the actual band values to open in QGIS. Week 1 tells you which settings to use.

Good to know

  • Websites change. If a button has moved, look for the same words nearby, or watch the video.
  • If an image looks white and blotchy, it is probably cloudy. Pick another date.
