Add a basemap

Put a street map or satellite map under your data for reference.

Time: about 15 minutesLevel: complete beginner

Watch

“Add Basemaps in Seconds with QuickMapServices (QGIS Tutorial)” by Alex Does GIS. Watch on YouTube.

More videos:

  • QGIS Basemaps: the Ultimate Guide (Geospatial School)

A basemap is a background map, such as a street map or satellite view, that helps you see where your data are. Basemaps are for reference only: you look at them, but you do not analyze them.

Option 1: OpenStreetMap (built in)

  1. In the Browser panel, expand XYZ Tiles.
  2. Double-click OpenStreetMap. It appears in the Layers panel.
  3. Drag it to the bottom of the Layers panel so your own data are drawn on top.

Option 2: More basemaps with the QuickMapServices plugin

  1. Install the QuickMapServices plugin (see Install a plugin).
  2. Go to Web › QuickMapServices and choose a map, for example OSM › OSM Standard.
  3. For many more choices, including satellite imagery, open Web › QuickMapServices › Settings, go to the More services tab, and click Get contributed pack.

Good to know

  • Basemaps need an internet connection. Without one, the map stays blank.
  • Basemaps are images streamed from the web. You cannot click on them to get pixel values or reflectance. For that, you need real satellite data, which you download in Weeks 1 and 3.
  • If you use a basemap in a published map, credit its source (for example, "© OpenStreetMap contributors").
