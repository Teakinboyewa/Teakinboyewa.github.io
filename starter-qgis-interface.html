Find your way around QGIS

The main parts of the QGIS window, and how to move around the map.

Time: about 25 minutesLevel: complete beginner

Watch

“Complete QGIS Interface Tutorial (2026)” by GeoSkill Pro. Watch on YouTube.

More videos:

  • QGIS Tutorials 02: Introduction to QGIS Interface for Absolute Beginners (Terra Spatial)

The main parts of the window

  • Menu bar (top): every tool in QGIS can be found here, under menus such as Project, Layer, Vector, and Raster.
  • Toolbars (under the menus): buttons for the tools you use most, such as zoom, pan, and select.
  • Browser panel (left): like a file explorer. Use it to find data on your computer and online sources.
  • Layers panel (left): the list of everything on your map. Layers at the top are drawn on top. Tick the box to show or hide a layer.
  • Map canvas (center): where your map appears.
  • Status bar (bottom): shows the coordinates under your mouse, the map scale, and the coordinate system of the project (for example EPSG:4326).
  • Processing Toolbox: a searchable list of hundreds of analysis tools. Open it with Processing › Toolbox.

Moving around the map

  • Zoom: roll the mouse wheel.
  • Pan (move): hold down the mouse wheel and drag, or hold the Space bar and move the mouse.
  • Zoom to a layer: right-click the layer in the Layers panel and choose Zoom to Layer(s).
  • See everything: click View › Zoom Full.

Try it

  1. Open QGIS and click Project › New.
  2. In the Browser panel, open XYZ Tiles and double-click OpenStreetMap. A world map appears.
  3. Zoom in to your town. Watch the coordinates change in the status bar as you move the mouse.
  4. Save the project with Project › Save As. QGIS projects end in .qgz.

If a panel disappears

It happens to everyone. Go to View › Panels and tick the panel you want back, such as Layers or Browser. Missing toolbars are under View › Toolbars.

Tip: a project file (.qgz) only remembers where your data are and how they look. It does not contain the data. If you move or delete your data files, the project cannot find them.
